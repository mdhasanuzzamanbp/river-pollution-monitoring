Fig 1: Spatial variations of the mean physiochemical profiles in the three segments of up, lower and downstream river system, Bangladesh. The bar associated with the mean values represent standard deviations. Different letters (a, b, and c) at the top mean statistically significant spatial differences of the metals based on one-way ANOVA test (p<0.05).
</p>
<br>
<div align="justify">
The basic statistics (mean and standard deviation) of physicochemical parameters in sediments are outlined in the table. The pH of the sediment varied within 6.5 to 7. Low pH in water can cause the release of absorbed metals, which are found in the upstream river (Mean: 6.23 ±0.76) because it reduces the strength of metal connection and interrupts the tension of metals in surface sediments. The maximum and minimum average pH values were estimated as 5.84-7.25 in the upstream river, 6.51-7.26 in the lower midstream river and 8.01-8.67 in the downstream river sediments, respectively. According to these findings, sediments were slightly alkaline to slightly acidic. The high percentage (%) of organic matter (OM) is detected in the upstream river (Mean: 2.63 ±0.54). The low percentage (%) of OM was found in the downstream river e.g., Pashur and Rupsha River (Mean: 1.32 ±0.91). The TOC in sediments was mainly averaged 1.87 ±0.54, 1.91 ±0.39 and 1.87 ±0.39 in upstream, lower midstream and downstream rivers, respectively.
</div>
